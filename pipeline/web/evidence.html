AI in education research: what kind of evidence is there?

An evidence read-out of the Stanford SCALE AI Hub research repository. SCALE curates the papers and tags them (application, user, age, purpose, design). Each paper's evidence profile (study type, what was measured, direction of the result, sample size, country, AI tool) is AI-extracted from its abstract and then audited by a second LLM pass. It describes what kind of evidence a paper reports, not how good it is. Read the papers before relying on them.
