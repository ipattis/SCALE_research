Trends

Ranked by change in each topic's share of the repository's papers, by when papers first appeared. The latest period is a partial year, so compare shares, not raw counts. Topics with few recent papers are marked Small. Click a topic to highlight its papers.

Where does my paper fit?

Paste a title and abstract (or a project description). It's embedded in your browser with Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B (a one-time ~570 MB model download, then cached) and placed next to its nearest papers in the repository. Nothing is sent to a server.
